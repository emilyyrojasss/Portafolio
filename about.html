Hi, I'm Emily...

The person behind the studio

I'm a brand & web designer who found my way here through a love of color, pattern and a good font pairing. What started as a curiosity for how a brand makes people feel turned into a full-time studio helping small businesses look as good as they are.

I specialize in crafting visual brand identities and marketing collateral for small businesses — the kind of work that gets you noticed for exactly the right reasons.

Why I Specialize in Creative Businesses

Creative entrepreneurs pour everything into their work — I love being the person who helps that work finally look like it feels. There's nothing better than a client seeing their new brand for the first time and saying, "that's exactly it."
